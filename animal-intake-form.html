How to collect a hair sample

  1. Pull 20–30 hairs from the mane or tail with the roots attached (do not cut).
  2. Pull 20–30 hairs from the base of the tail or along the back with the roots attached (do not cut).
  3. Place the hair in a labeled ziplock bag with your animal's name and your name written on it.
  4. Place the ziplock bag inside a padded envelope for mailing.
