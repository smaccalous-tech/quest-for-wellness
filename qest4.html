The science
What is bioenergetic testing?

Bioenergetic testing uses a system that outputs electromagnetic frequencies and views the response of the innate intelligence. In this way, we are able to communicate with the body's innate intelligence to enhance energetic flow by providing "corrected" information. Rather than getting distracted by seemingly complex symptoms, bioenergetic testing seeks root-cause issues.

The technology behind Qest4 stems from the work of Dr. Reinhold Voll, a German physician who developed electroacupuncture in the 1950s. Voll's work focused on measuring electrical conductance at acupuncture points to assess organ function. Qest4 takes this concept further with a computerized system to scan for energetic imbalances throughout the body.

The QEST4 software contains digitally-encoded information with thousands of energetic signatures relating to health-related factors — ranging across anatomy and physiology, body chemistry, medicines, nutrients and pathogens, mental and emotional factors, metabolic processes, and more.

During a test, a set of "signatures" are output in rapid succession by the QEST4 hardware as electromagnetic signals. Using a simple and safe low-voltage circuit formed by holding two brass cylinders, the response of the body to those signals is recorded. The response being measured is small changes in the electrical resistance of the skin. This information is relayed back to the software and a report is generated.

The Qest4 system communicates with the innate intelligence — or energy, chi, spirit — through informational energetic patterns, and receives a response from the body through the electrodes. This transference of information through energetic language is how everything becomes interconnected. With Qest4, you are able to find the bigger picture and see causes of imbalances instead of endlessly chasing symptoms.

There is compelling evidence that any physical imbalances first present themselves in the energetic body. By rebalancing at an energetic level, physical ailments and disease are less likely to manifest. Wellness begins at an energetic level.

Your session
What happens during a Qest4 session?

Sessions are relaxed, comfortable, and completely non-invasive. Most appointments are 60–90 minutes. Here's exactly what to expect.

1

Initial consultation

Kelly reviews your intake form and has an in-depth conversation about your health concerns, medical history, lifestyle, and goals to tailor the session to your unique needs.

2

Preparation

You are connected to the Qest4 device via metal rods that you hold. These send and receive low-level electrical signals. The process is completely painless and non-invasive — no needles, no blood draws.

3

Scanning

The Qest4 system scans your body's energy fields by sending specific frequencies through the body and measuring your response. Several scans may be run depending on your concerns and what prior scans reveal.

4

Analysis

The Qest4 software analyzes the data and generates a detailed report highlighting areas where your body may be experiencing stress — potentially identifying food sensitivities, nutritional deficiencies, environmental toxins, or emotional blockages.

5

Discussion of results

Kelly goes over the findings with you in plain language, explaining what the results mean and how they relate to your overall health. This is your opportunity to ask questions and gain real insight.

6

Personalized recommendations

Based on your scan results, Kelly creates a personalized wellness plan — which may include dietary changes, nutritional supplements, homeopathic remedies, imprinted tincture drops, or lifestyle adjustments.

7

Imprinting (if applicable)

Imprinting transfers balancing energetic signatures into a digitally encodable carrier — such as liquid drops, crystals, patches, essential oils, or lasers. These mediums hold energetic patterns to support the energetic rebalancing process.

8

Follow-up appointments

Regular follow-ups are highly recommended to monitor progress and adjust your wellness plan. Over time the goal is to restore balance to your body's energy fields and improve your overall health and vitality.
