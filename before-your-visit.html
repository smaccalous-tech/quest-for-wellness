Day of your appointment
What to bring & how to prepare

Bring your supplements

Bring any supplements you are currently taking and want tested. The Qest4 can assess how your body responds to your current supplement protocol.

Stay hydrated beforehand

Drink plenty of water in the 24 hours prior to your appointment. For bioenergetic testing, it is helpful to be well hydrated for the most accurate results.

No lotion on your hands

Do not apply lotion to your hands prior to your appointment. Lotion can interfere with the electrical conductance needed for accurate bioenergetic testing.

No fasting required

You do not need to fast before testing. Eat normally on the day of your appointment — there are no dietary restrictions before your session.

Complete your intake form

Have your intake form filled out prior to your appointment. You can complete it online at questforwellnessllc.com/intake-form or email it to QuestforWellness22@gmail.com before your visit.

Contraindications — please read

Please let Kelly know prior to your appointment if you have any of the following, as they are contraindicated with Biofeedback testing:

Stints more than 5 years old
Organ transplants with immunosuppressant drugs
Shunts of any type
Medicated for seizures
Pacemakers

Note: Metal plates, pins, rods, and denture implants are NOT contraindicated with Biofeedback services.
